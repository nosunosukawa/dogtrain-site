PraisePup Privacy Policy

PraisePup (ホメなう。) collects nothing. Your log stays on your device. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of ours.

Last updated: September 28, 2026 ・ Operator: Nobutaka Kawasaki

Information we collect

None. The app uses no analytics, crash reporting, advertising identifiers or third-party SDKs that collect data.

What is stored on your device

  • Your dog's name, age and potty spot (if you enter them)
  • Your potty log (times of successes, accidents and no-gos), each day's result and the current step
  • Whether reminders are on

This stays inside the app on your device and is never sent to the developer. It is included in your own iPhone backups (including iCloud Backup), which only you control.

Notifications

If you allow them, the app schedules a reminder for the next potty trip on your iPhone. Nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn reminders off in Settings.

Deleting your data

Use "Start over" at the bottom of Settings in the app, or delete the app.

What leaves your device

  • When you tap a source link, this policy or the support page, Safari opens that page. Your log is not sent.
  • When you export your log as CSV, it goes only where you choose in the iOS share sheet.

Email

If you email us, your address and message are used only to reply.

Children

The app is for dog owners. It collects no information from anyone, including children.
