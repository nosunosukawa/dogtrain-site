Support

Questions or bug reports: nosunosukawa@gmail.com. You don't need to include your dog's name or other personal details.

Common questions

I forgot to tap "End the day"

That's fine. The next time you open the app, the previous day is scored from your log.

The app moved me back a step

Two or more accidents in a day means dropping back one step. If the same step drops back twice, it is split in half.

I get no reminders

Check that notifications for PraisePup are allowed in the iPhone Settings app, and that "Notify me at the next trip time" is on in the app's Settings. Only the next reminder is scheduled, and it stops when you end the day.

Exporting or deleting my log

Use "Export log as CSV" or "Start over" in the app's Settings.

When to see a vet

See a vet before training if accidents suddenly increase, your dog goes very often, there is blood in the urine or a strong smell, or your dog drinks a lot of water. Growling, biting, strong fear, or problems only when left alone are outside this app's scope; talk to your vet or a veterinary behaviorist.
